Citation Help

Using APA 7th Edition

Use the following examples as a quick guide when completing your reference list and citing research on the other pages.

Journal Article

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. Journal Title, volume(issue), page range. https://doi.org/xxxxx

Organisation Report

Organisation Name. (Year). Title of report. URL

Webpage

Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of webpage. Website Name. URL
