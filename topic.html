The research topic

Education in a changing world

The pandemic created an urgent need for schools and universities to use digital technologies to continue teaching and learning when face-to-face classes were disrupted.

Topic background

Why study online learning during COVID-19?

COVID-19 significantly disrupted education around the world. In New Zealand, schools and universities had to adapt to changing restrictions and find ways to support learning remotely. Online learning became an important way to maintain educational activities during periods when traditional classroom learning was not possible.

A rapid transition

Teachers and students had to adjust to learning and teaching through digital platforms, often within a short period. This raised questions about access to technology, teaching approaches, and the effectiveness of remote learning.

Different experiences

Online learning did not affect everyone in the same way. Students' home environments, access to devices and internet, and levels of support could influence their learning experiences.

Lessons for the future

Studying this period can help us understand the benefits and limitations of digital learning and how education systems might prepare for future disruptions.

Key areas of investigation

Three areas we are exploring

Our research focuses on the experiences of students and teachers, as well as differences in access and opportunities across communities.

01

Student Engagement, Outcomes & Wellbeing

Exploring how online learning affected students' participation, motivation, learning outcomes, and wellbeing during the pandemic.

02

Teachers' Adapting Methods & Digital Tools

Investigating how teachers adapted their teaching methods, used digital platforms, and supported students in remote learning environments.

03

Inequality Across Students & Communities

Examining how differences in internet access, devices, learning environments, and available support may have affected students and communities.

Guiding question

How did COVID-19 change education in New Zealand?

What were the major benefits and challenges of online learning, and how did the transition affect students, teachers, and education?

Research aim

Examine the impact

Our aim is to examine how the shift to online learning affected education in New Zealand during the COVID-19 pandemic, identifying its major benefits, challenges, and implications for students, teachers, and communities.
