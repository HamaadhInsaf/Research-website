Project overview

Understanding the shift to online learning

COVID-19 forced schools and universities across New Zealand to rapidly move from face-to-face teaching to online learning, creating new opportunities as well as significant challenges.

01

Students

Student engagement, educational outcomes, learning experiences, and wellbeing during the shift to online learning.

02

Teachers

How teachers adapted their teaching methods and made use of digital tools in a rapidly changing environment.

03

Inequality

Differences in access, experiences, and challenges across students and communities.

Guiding question

How did COVID-19 change education in New Zealand?

What were the major benefits and challenges of online learning, and how did the transition affect students, teachers, and education?
